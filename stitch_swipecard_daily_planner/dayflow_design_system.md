# DayFlow Design System

## Visual Identity
- **Palette**:
  - Background: `#F7F5F0` (Creamy Paper)
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
  - Accent (Primary): `#2D5A3D` (Forest Green)
  - Accent (Secondary): `#C8622A` (Terracotta)
  - Text: `#1A1714` (Deep Charcoal)
  - Muted: `#7A7168` (Warm Grey)
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: 'Playfair Display', serif (Elegant, classic)
  - Body: 'DM Sans', sans-serif (Modern, readable)

## Component Patterns
- **Swipe Cards**: Full-viewport cards with rounded corners, subtle shadows, and centered content. Transition animations for swiping.
- **Sticky Notes**: Varied pastel backgrounds, handwritten-style typography for accents, and slight rotation for a physical "pinned" look.
- **Progress Indicator**: Minimalist dots at the bottom of the card stack.

## Interaction Model
1. **The Flow**: User enters the daily flow.
2. **The Cards**: One card per section (Priorities, To-Do, Meals, Water, etc.). Swiping left/right navigates.
3. **The Reveal**: After the final card, a "See My Day" button leads to the sticky note dashboard.
